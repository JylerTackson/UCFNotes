
### Definition 3.1 - Norm
A *norm* on a vector space $V$ is a function
$$
\begin{align}
\tag{3.1}
||\cdot||:V\to&\;\mathbb{R},\\
x\mapsto &\; ||x||
\tag{3.2}
\end{align}
$$ which assigns each vector $x$ its length $||x||\in\mathbb{R}$, such that for all $\lambda\in\mathbb{R}$ and $x,y\in V$ the following hold:
1) Absolutely homogenous: $||\lambda x|| = |\lambda |\;||x||$
2) Triangle inequality: $||x+y|| \leq ||x|| + ||y||$
3) Positive definite: $||x|| \geq 0$ and $||x|| = 0 \Longleftrightarrow x=0$
