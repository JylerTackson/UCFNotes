A transformer, is a model architecture [^1]eschewing recurrence and instead relying entirely on an attention mechanism to draw global dependencies between input and output. 

Self attention is an attention mechanism relating different positions of a single sequence in order to compute a representation of the sequence; for ex. a sentence and trying to relate the words in the sentence or a picture and relating the pixels in the image.
